What you can set up

Levels and role rewards

Run independent XP systems with their own channel rules, cooldowns, and leaderboards. Award Discord roles when members reach milestones.

Custom economies

Give each currency its own earning rules and balances. Let members earn, bank, and transfer currency or spend it in your shop.

Moderation and AutoMod

Warn or time out members, keep a case history, and set consequences for repeat warnings. Use Discord AutoMod to block spam, keywords, and excessive mentions.

Welcome cards and server tools

Welcome new members with a card and automatic roles. Compose embeds and send Creator Alerts to a channel when tracked creators post or go live.

Set up your server

Sign in with Discord, choose a server you manage, and configure the features you want to use.
